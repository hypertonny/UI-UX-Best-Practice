# DailyToGo — Master Animation & GIF Asset Guide (VBU ⇄ Karjat Corridor)

> **Visual Theme & Style Directive**:  
> Based on the official **Bus Graphic Style Directive**:
> - **Chassis**: Vibrant Electric Blue (`#0091EA`) with thick white contour outer border.
> - **Windows**: Bright Amber-Orange (`#FF9100` / `#FFA000`) with crisp diagonal white reflection glare lines.
> - **Wheels**: Electric Blue rims with bold white outer contour and white hubcap details.
> - **Transit Corridor**: Dedicated student shuttle service connecting **Vijaybhoomi University (VBU at Solanpada)** and **Karjat S.T. Stand (Depot / Railway Station)**.
> - **Strict Rule**: Flat 2D vector styling, **ABSOLUTELY ZERO GRADIENTS**, zero 3D renders, and zero emojis.

---

## 🗺️ Core App Concept: Direct 2-Stop Point-to-Point Shuttle

The motive of the app is strictly a **direct, non-stop campus university shuttle** with **only two stops**:
1. **Karjat S.T. Stand (Depot / Railway Station)** — Departure & Arrival hub connecting Mumbai & Pune suburban trains.
2. **Solanpada ST Bus Stop (Vijaybhoomi University Campus Gate)** — Direct campus terminal.

There are **no intermediate boarding or drop stops**. The shuttle runs directly between these two points across a scenic 24 km ghat highway corridor (~45 min transit time).
