01 · Research & Empathy

The Rural Ghat "Information Vacuum"

02 / 07

1. The Rural Ghat Road

VBU is 24 km away from Karjat station. It takes about 1 hour 30 minutes along a winding mountain highway with frequent network blind spots.

2. Zero GPS Tracking

Government buses and contracted shuttles have no GPS installed. Students never know if the bus is 5 minutes away or has already passed.

3. The Waiting Dip

Our 7-stage commuter journey showed that student anxiety peaks while waiting at the gate due to a total lack of feedback.

02 · Design Evolution: Wireframe ➔ Code

Figma Wireframes vs. What We Built Different

03 / 07

🔴 Figma Wireframe (Before)

  • Static Timing: Plain text box showing fixed departure time with no live countdown.
  • Cluttered Map: Displayed all 21 village stops, overwhelming students who only travel point-to-point.
  • Hidden Actions: No direction swap button; required going through menus to reverse route.
  • No Fault Handling: Assumed 100% network connectivity in the mountain ghat pass.

🟢 Live Production App (After)

  • Live Clock Ticker: Real-time ticking countdown (`BOARDING NOW` / `14m 22s`) synced to device clock.
  • 7 Key Corridor Halts: Shows major stops (Char Rasta, D-Mart, Bhivpuri, Kashele, Jambrung) with ETA.
  • 1-Tap Thumb Zone: Sticky direction swapper and "Show Pass" button right on the main screen.
  • Multilingual Engine: Instant 1-tap toggle for English, Hindi (`हिं`), and Marathi (`म`).
Design Evolution Pitch: "Moving from Figma to code, we added live clock tickers, 7 key corridor halts, and live Hindi/Marathi language support."

04 · Engineering & System Architecture

How Live Tracking Works Without Bus GPS

05 / 07

User Question Raised in Testing

"Government buses don't have college GPS chips. How will the live tracking actually work?"

Instead of waiting for expensive bus hardware, we built a Dual-Source Software Model.

Dual-Source Tracking Model

  • 1. Driver Web Beacon (Primary): The driver runs a simple web screen on their dashboard that pings coordinates every 10 seconds.
  • 2. Passenger Mesh (Crowdsourcing): When students on the bus confirm their trip, their phones provide opt-in location pings.
  • 3. Offline Fallback: If 4G drops in the mountain pass, the app smoothly switches to the scheduled timetable with an "offline estimate" status.
System Architecture Pitch: "We solved the GPS problem with driver phone beacons and student crowdsourcing—zero hardware cost."
