UX CASE STUDY · CAMPUS TRANSIT DESIGN

Dailytogo — Next-Gen Student Transit App

Eliminating the 90-minute rural information vacuum between Vijaybhoomi University (Solanpada) and Karjat Railway Station through real-time transit telemetry and 1-click information architecture.

Designer: Rahul Purohit Course: Human Computer Interaction (STET301) Institution: Vijaybhoomi University Timeline: Aug – Sep 2026
01 · The Context & Dilemma

The 24 km Ghat Highway Information Vacuum

Vijaybhoomi University is situated in Jambrung, 24.2 km away from Karjat Railway Station along an isolated mountain pass. Over 1,200 students, faculty, and operational staff depend on government ST buses and campus shuttles that operate with zero live tracking.

45–90 Min Delays

Students wait at rural roadside stops without knowing if the bus has broken down, is stuck in mountain traffic, or has already departed.

Zero Bus Hardware

State-run government transport buses lack GPS hardware integrations. Any solution must work purely through software.

Strict Point-to-Point

Students only travel between Karjat S.T. Depot and Solanpada (VBU Gate). Intermediate stops cause confusion if highlighted.

02 · User Research & Empathy

Mapping the Commuter Emotional Curve

We tracked students across 7 journey stages: Awakening ➔ Packing ➔ Reaching Gate ➔ Waiting Stage ➔ Boarding ➔ Ghat Transit ➔ Karjat Arrival. Student emotional satisfaction drops to its lowest level during the waiting stage due to lack of system feedback.

05 · Design Evolution: Wireframe ➔ Production

Figma Wireframes vs. Interactive Mobile Reality

Bridging the gap between conceptual Figma artboards and real mobile code. Deploying on physical mobile viewports exposed friction in transit timing, navigation depth, and network fault tolerance that reshaped the entire architecture.

Screen 1: Quick Glance & Countdown

Primary touchpoint for 90% of students checking bus status between classes.

Figma Wireframe (Before)
  • Static text box with fixed, non-ticking departure time.
  • Static gray placeholder box for bus graphic.
  • No direction swapping; required visiting a sub-menu.
  • No digital pass quick-access for gate checks.
Live Production App (After)
  • Live Ticking Countdown (e.g. 14m 22s / BOARDING NOW) synced to device clock.
  • 1-Tap Direction Inverter swaps Solanpada ⇄ Karjat instantly.
  • 1-Tap "Show Pass" CTA elevated into primary thumb zone.
  • Muted Campus Video Stream with live telemetry badge.

Screen 2: Live Tracking & Route Progression

Telemetry radar and corridor stop progression along the 24 km ghat road.

Figma Wireframe (Before)
  • Cluttered route view displaying all 21 village stops as identical pins.
  • Caused visual overwhelm for students who only travel point-to-point.
  • Assumed 100% active GPS without network loss handling.
Live Production App (After)
  • 7 Key Corridor Halts (Karjat Depot, Char Rasta, D-Mart, Bhivpuri, Kashele, Jambrung, Solanpada VBU Gate) with real-time ETA.
  • 21 total village stops preserved in an expandable secondary drawer.
  • Multilingual Engine: 1-tap live switcher for English, Hindi, and Marathi.
  • Location-Off Error Overlay with 1-tap retry state for ghat dead zones.

Screen 3: Timings & Departure Timetable

Authoritative departure and arrival schedule across all 10 daily corridor runs.

Figma Wireframe (Before)
  • Flat static text list with identical cards across all hours.
  • No visual cue for the next upcoming bus; forced users to calculate manually.
  • Uncalibrated trip durations ranging from 15 min to 2.5 hours.
Live Production App (After)
  • Clock-Aware Highlighting automatically tags the active bus with a glowing NEXT BUS badge.
  • Calibrated 1h 30m realistic travel time across all daily trips.
  • Interactive bell reminder alarm toggles with system toasts.
  • Integrated campus terminal boarding video stream.

Screen 4: Student Account & Bus Pass

Digital student identity card, semester transit pass, and file validation.

Figma Wireframe (Before)
  • Basic user profile form with standard text inputs.
  • No security credentials or official transit identification pass.
  • Generic photo upload button without file size constraints.
Live Production App (After)
  • Digital Student ID Card with RFID code and semester validity pill.
  • Interactive 5MB photo upload validator with error and success toast simulation.
  • Direct emergency contact links to Campus Security & Transit Dispatch.

Screen 5: Transit Alerts & Advisories

Urgent safety broadcasts and campus transit operations notifications.

Figma Wireframe (Before)
  • Generic notification list populated with promotional discount text.
  • No visual urgency hierarchy or distinction between news and safety.
Live Production App (After)
  • Contextual Weather Warning for heavy monsoon ghat rainfall and landslide caution.
  • Night Library Shuttle Alerts for late-hour campus study periods.
  • Persistent header bell with unread badge count and 1-tap back navigation.

06 · Comparative Analysis

Benchmarked Against 3 Classmate Projects

We evaluated DailyToGo against peers in student logistics to balance simplicity against feature depth:

Kunal & Ganesh (Ride Pool)

Their Model: Splitting auto/cab costs via chat.
UX Takeaway: High cognitive overhead. Validated that DailyToGo must remain zero-friction (check bus in 1s).

Prasad (Lost & Found)

Their Model: Web reporting directory.
UX Takeaway: Took 4–5 clicks to reach posting form. Reinforced DailyToGo's 1-tap thumb zone architecture.

Neermay (Hostel Alert)

Their Model: Single-touch broadcast alert.
UX Takeaway: Harsh red-and-white theme caused eye fatigue. Proven advantage of DailyToGo's calibrated dark mode.
