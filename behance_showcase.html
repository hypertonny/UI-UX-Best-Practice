UX CASE STUDY · CAMPUS TRANSIT DESIGN

Dailytogo — Student Transit App

Eliminating the 90-minute rural information vacuum between Vijaybhoomi University (Solanpada) and Karjat Railway Station with real-time transit telemetry, 7 key corridor halts, and 1-click information architecture.

Designer: Rahul Purohit Course: Human Computer Interaction (STET301) Institution: Vijaybhoomi University Timeline: Aug – Sep 2026
01 · CONTEXT & PROBLEM

The 24.2 km Mountain Ghat Commute Vacuum

Vijaybhoomi University is situated in Jambrung, 24.2 km away from Karjat Railway Station. Over 1,200 students, faculty, and operational staff depend on government ST buses and campus shuttles with zero real-time visibility.

45–90 Min Delays

Students wait at roadside stops without knowing if the bus has broken down, is delayed in mountain traffic, or has already departed.

Zero Bus Hardware

State-run buses lack university GPS trackers. Solved through a Dual-Source Software Model: Driver web beacon + passenger crowdsource mesh.

7 Key Corridor Halts

Covers the primary student halts (Karjat Depot, Char Rasta, D-Mart, Bhivpuri, Kashele, Jambrung, VBU Gate) with ~1h 30m calibrated travel time.

Screen 🔴 Figma Wireframe (Before) 🟢 Live Production App (After) UX Rationale & Heuristic
Screen 1: Quick Static departure text box; no countdown; no direction swap; no pass button. Live Ticking Countdown (e.g. 14m 22s), 1-tap direction inverter, 1-tap pass display. Heuristic #1 (Visibility of Status): Eliminates doubt with real-time feedback.
Screen 2: Live Cluttered map with all 21 village pins; assumed 100% active GPS connectivity. 7 Key Corridor Halts with relative ETAs; Location-Off Error Overlay with 1-tap retry. Hick's Law & Heuristic #9: Removes cognitive clutter and recovers from ghat dead zones.
Screen 3: Timetable Flat static list; identical rows; uncalibrated trip times (15m to 2.5h). Clock-Aware Highlighting with glowing NEXT BUS pill; calibrated 1h 30m travel time. Heuristic #6 (Recognition over Recall): System identifies next bus automatically.
Screen 4: Account Basic profile text form; no official transit verification pass. Digital Student ID Card with RFID code, semester pill, and 5MB photo upload validation. Heuristic #5 (Error Prevention): Instant gate security verification.
Screen 5: Alerts Generic promotional list ("10% off festive exam discount"). Contextual Safety Feed: Monsoon landslide warnings, night library shuttle operational alerts. Heuristic #8 (Minimalist Design): Focuses strictly on commuter safety.

07 · PEER BENCHMARKING

Benchmarked Against 3 Classmate Projects

Evaluated DailyToGo against peers in student logistics to balance simplicity against feature depth:

Kunal & Ganesh (Ride Pool)

Their Model: Splitting auto/cab costs via chat.
UX Takeaway: High cognitive overhead. Validated that DailyToGo must remain 1-click simple (check bus in 1s).

Prasad (Lost & Found)

Their Model: Web reporting directory.
UX Takeaway: Took 4–5 clicks to reach posting form. Reinforced DailyToGo's 1-tap thumb zone architecture.

Neermay (Hostel Alert)

Their Model: Single-touch broadcast alert.
UX Takeaway: Harsh red-and-white theme caused eye fatigue. Proven advantage of DailyToGo's calibrated dark mode.
